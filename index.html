Staff Protocols & Guidelines

Complete rulebook for application terms, staff behaviors, tickets, and everyday obligations.

1. Fast Staff Application Rules
  • Do not DM staff members asking to check your application status.
  • Providing false information or fake IDs results in an immediate blacklisting.
  • Must wait at least 2 weeks before re-applying if denied.
  • Applicants must maintain a clean warning history inside the community.
2. Staff Rules & Conduct
  • Never abuse commands (mutes, bans, kicks) for personal amusement.
  • Treat every server member with equal respect without bias or favoritism.
  • Keep all staff discussions, logs, and vote channels strictly confidential.
  • Remain objective and calm during intense arguments or raids.
3. Ticket Management System Rules
  • Prompt Claims: Claim open tickets efficiently; avoid making users wait excessively long.
  • Professional Greeting: Always use formal welcoming templates when addressing user support inquiries.
  • Inactivity Protocol: Ping users after 24 hours of silence and close unresolved dormant tickets after 48 hours.
  • Escalations: Hand over severe security risks, raids, or financial grievances directly to senior admins.
4. Core Staff Duties & Daily Actions
  • Chat Oversight: Keep chat channels clean from spam, toxicity, and unauthorized advertising.
  • Voice Assistance: Supervise public voice channels and prevent audio disruption or harassment.
  • Evidence Logging: Screenshot and save proof before carrying out severe infractions like bans.
